Music & Moving Image

A strong instinct for the emotional interplay of image and music.

He develops audiovisual scenes through the combined rhythm of editing, composition and sound design – using music not merely as accompaniment but as an integral part of cinematic storytelling.

Music is composed and placed to:

  • Heighten emotion
  • Create atmosphere
  • Build tension
  • Shape narrative rhythm
  • Carry transitions
  • Define characters musically
  • Connect scenes emotionally
  • Intensify editing and image
