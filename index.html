The Trilogy

Part 1/ 3

UNDER THE DARKHow It All Began

Bulgaria, today. Ten strangers who know each other only from small windows on a screen book a six-day trek into the Rhodope Mountains, to three villages where everyone has vanished. Their guide lost the woman he loved in Mexico five years ago, to something that came up from under the earth. He knows the signs. He tells no one. By the time he does, only the light decides who comes home.

Feature film · approx. 100 minutes · Sci-fi horror drama

Part 2/ 3

UNDER THE DARKThe Invasion

Ten years later, lights appear behind the moon, hundreds of them, and they are moving. Motherships hang over every great city, and a Dome closes around the Earth until the last daylight is gone. A mother crosses a dark Europe for thirty days with her ten-year-old daughter, heading home to the mountains, where a man has found a cave in which people can survive, if they learn to hide their fear.

Feature film · approx. 130 minutes · Sci-fi drama

Part 3/ 3

UNDER THE DARKThe Sun Warriors

For more than two hundred years the Earth has lain beneath a Dome that lets no light through. Fourteen people from a cave in the mountains set out to bring back the sun. They follow a man who once served the Beings himself, and one of them is a traitor.

Feature film · approx. 155 minutes · Sci-fi epic

The Beings

They are deaf. Their white eyes see shape and movement in the dark, as clearly as we see by day. A third eye on the forehead sees something else: fear.

Light in their eyes stuns them for a few seconds. Fire hurts them, but it does not stop them. Those few seconds are all you get.

Stay in the light.

The Author

Nedy John Cross is an award-winning writer, director, producer and composer based in Berlin. Born in Bulgaria and raised in Germany, he trained in classical music and spent many years on stage as a rock musician before turning to film. He writes, directs, produces and scores his own projects through his production company Filmpartner24 and his studio in Berlin.

UNDER THE DARK is a story he has carried since childhood. Now is the time to tell it as a trilogy. A story of light and fear, across two hundred years and three generations.

It begins in our time, in Bulgaria’s Rhodope Mountains. A mountain guide who lost the woman he loved in Mexico leads ten strangers to three villages where everyone has vanished. He knows the signs, and he keeps silent too long. Ten years later a Dome closes around the Earth, the last daylight dies, and a mother carries her daughter for thirty days through a dark Europe, home to the mountains. Two hundred years after that, fourteen people who have never seen the sun leave their cave city to bring it back.

The Beings are deaf. They see in the dark, and with their third eye they see fear. Light stuns them for a few seconds. Everything grows from that: what people teach each other to survive. How to breathe so they cannot find you. How to hold the light. Whose hand you never let go. What ten strangers learn in the mountains in the first film becomes the rule of a community in the second and, in the third, the inheritance that saves the world. A compass, a lamp and a braid pass from hand to hand across two centuries.

Three films in three registers: a horror drama in the mountains, a flight through the end of the world, an epic across half the planet. Each stands on its own. Together they tell what people do for each other in the dark.
