UNDER THE DARK

Three Films. Three Generations. A World Without Light.

What happens to humanity when the sun disappears and the darkness is no longer empty?

A story that begins in the remote mountains of Bulgaria, plunges the entire Earth into darkness, and more than two hundred years later leads to a final battle for the future of humanity.

Part 1

UNDER THE DARKHow It All Began

Mysterious events are unfolding in Bulgaria's enigmatic Rhodope Mountains. People vanish without a trace, entire villages stand abandoned, and no one can explain what is hiding deep within these remote mountains.

A mountain guide who lost the woman he loved during an expedition in Mexico years earlier leads ten strangers into this unsettling region. But what begins as a search for answers turns into a nightmare beyond human imagination.

Deep within the mountains lurk extraterrestrial beings driven by a horrifying necessity: To survive, they depend on a hormone produced by the human body under extreme fear.

To these creatures, humans are not enemies. They are a vital resource, a harvest.

Their captives are imprisoned inside glass containers and systematically subjected to mortal terror. The hormone released by their bodies is extracted and used by the aliens to sustain their own lives.

The creatures are deaf, yet they can see even in complete darkness and detect the fear of their victims through a third eye. Against these almost silent hunters, the group is drawn into a merciless struggle for survival.

In abandoned villages, dark forests, and deep cave systems, the humans fight an enemy that is physically far superior to them. Every mistake could be fatal. Fear becomes their greatest danger, and overcoming it their only chance.

But what the survivors discover in the mountains is only a small part of a far greater threat.

For Earth has already become the target of an extraterrestrial civilization.

Part 2

UNDER THE DARKThe Invasion

Ten years after the horrifying events in Bulgaria, the unthinkable happens.

The extraterrestrial beings return to Earth. But this time, they do not come in secret. They come to take control of the entire planet.

A gigantic dome envelops the Earth, cutting humanity off from sunlight. The world descends into eternal darkness.

Civilization collapses. Cities become ruins, governments lose control, and humanity falls prey to an extraterrestrial power that seeks not merely to destroy its victims, but to exploit them as a vital resource.

The invaders rule the darkness. They are deaf, yet they can see without light and detect human fear through their third eye. Millions of people become prisoners of a merciless harvesting system.

Amid this apocalyptic world, a mother struggles to bring her daughter through a devastated Europe and back to their homeland in the mountains of Bulgaria.

For thirty days, their journey takes them through abandoned cities, ravaged landscapes, and territories where humanity has long since lost control.

Their path becomes a desperate struggle against extraterrestrial hunters, the cruelty of a collapsing society, and the limits of their own endurance.

As the old world dies, their story becomes a symbol of what drives human beings even in the face of total annihilation: love, courage, and the hope of a future.

Part 3

UNDER THE DARKThe Sun Warriors

Two hundred years have passed.

For humanity, the sun has become nothing more than a legend. Generations have been born and died without ever seeing daylight.

Deep beneath the Earth, the last survivors have established a new civilization. But on the surface, a world exists that bears almost no resemblance to the Earth that once was.

The darkness has changed everything.

Mutants roam the devastated landscapes. Strange creatures dominate the ruins of the old world. Over two centuries, Earth has transformed into a hostile planet where extraterrestrial rulers and countless mutated life forms have made human survival nearly impossible.

For those living underground, the surface is a place of terror, known only through legends and warnings.

But fourteen people decide to attempt the impossible.

They leave the safety of their underground city and embark on an expedition whose purpose is greater than their own survival: They intend to destroy the enormous dome with which the extraterrestrial rulers have kept Earth in eternal darkness for two centuries. Their mission is to free humanity from the darkness and restore sunlight to the planet.

Their journey takes them through a world filled with mutants, extraterrestrial beings, deadly dangers, and forgotten secrets. Along the way, they uncover traces of a past that connects the fate of their ancestors to their own future.

What began more than two hundred years earlier in the mountains of Bulgaria now reaches its decisive turning point.

The descendants of the survivors become the Sun Warriors.

And the struggle for survival becomes a battle for the light.

An Epic Of Darkness And Hope

UNDER THE DARK brings together horror, science fiction, post-apocalyptic drama, and adventure in an epic film trilogy spanning three generations.

Three films. Three different worlds. One story unfolding across more than two hundred years.

A story of fear and survival, of love and sacrifice, of the collapse of a civilization and humanity's unbreakable determination to reclaim its future.

For even after two hundred years of darkness, the hope of light has never died.

The Creatures

They can see your fear.

They are nomads of the universe. An ancient extraterrestrial civilization that has wandered through the vastness of space for countless generations, forever searching for new life.

But wherever they appear, they leave no civilizations behind. They leave dead worlds.

Planet after planet, entire species fall victim to them. Not out of hatred, not out of a desire for conquest, but out of a cruel necessity. Their survival depends on a hormone produced by living beings in moments of extreme mortal fear.

They hunt, they harvest, and they move on. Only when the last living creature has been exterminated and a planet completely depleted does their search begin again.

Countless worlds have already fallen to them. Civilizations whose stories will never be told again. Planets where life once flourished and where now only silence remains.

Now they have reached Earth.

And for the first time in human history, humanity faces an enemy that sees it as nothing more than the next harvest.

The Hunters of Darkness

The creatures are deaf. They cannot hear voices or screams. Their white eyes can detect every movement and every shape, even in complete darkness. Yet their perception is fundamentally different from that of humans. They see their surroundings entirely in black and white, a colorless world of shadows, outlines, and movement.

Until a human being feels fear.

Then the third eye on their forehead awakens.

Against the black-and-white surroundings, a human silhouette suddenly appears in brilliant red. The stronger the fear, the more intense the red becomes. Panic transforms a person into a glowing signal, standing out unmistakably from everything around them.

They do not merely see the human being. They see the fear within.

Those who panic reveal themselves. Those who cannot control their fear become prey.

Their Greatest Strength Is Their Weakness

The creatures possess superior physical strength, move almost silently, and dominate the darkness. Direct light shining into their white eyes can paralyze them for a few seconds. Fire can injure them, but even severe burns may not be enough to stop them.

Yet even these seemingly invincible hunters have a weakness.

Their third eye.

Located in the center of their forehead, it glows with an eerie red light. It is the source of their ability to perceive human fear and, at the same time, their most sensitive and vulnerable point.

The very ability that gives them an almost insurmountable advantage during the hunt could ultimately become their downfall.

But to strike this weakness, a human must stand face to face with the creature. A being capable of detecting even the slightest trace of fear.

A single moment of panic can be fatal.

You can hide. You can run. You can fight.

But you must never show fear.

Stay in the light.

The Author

Nedy John Cross comes from an artistic family with deep roots in the theater. An award-winning writer, director, producer, and composer, he was born in Bulgaria, raised in Germany, and currently lives in Berlin.

His artistic journey began with classical music training. He spent many years performing on stage as a rock musician before discovering his passion for cinema and dedicating himself to filmmaking.

Through his Bulgarian production company Filmpartner24, his U.S.-based film company Planet Earth Films LLC, and his film studio in Berlin, he has established an international production structure. As a writer, director, producer, and composer, he brings together the essential creative disciplines of his film projects under one vision — from the initial concept and direction to the musical composition.

Over the years, Nedy John Cross has built an international network of filmmakers, creative professionals, and production partners, providing a strong foundation for his ambitious film and television projects.

UNDER THE DARK is a story he has carried since childhood. Now is the time to tell it as a trilogy. A story of light and fear, across two hundred years and three generations.
