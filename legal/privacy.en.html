2. Hosting and server log files

The website is delivered via Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, Cloudflare automatically processes technical data sent by your browser (IP address, date and time, page requested, referrer, browser and operating system) in order to deliver the site and protect it against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes this data as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR on the basis of its Data Processing Addendum. For transfers to the USA, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition. The data is stored only as long as necessary for secure operation.

3. Fonts

All fonts are loaded from our own server. There is no connection to Google or other font providers.

4. Language selection, no tracking cookies

When you open the home page, the language is chosen automatically based on the country you are visiting from (Bulgaria: Bulgarian; Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein: German; all other countries: English). Our hosting provider Cloudflare derives the country from your IP address; nothing is stored in the process. If you choose a language yourself in the language bar, we store this choice in a cookie ("twn_lang", language code only, valid for 12 months) so that you are not redirected again. This cookie is necessary for the function you requested. Apart from this, we do not use cookies, analytics services or advertising pixels. Links to sources and other websites are plain links: no data is transferred until you click a link.

5. Film and music trailers (YouTube)

Some articles show official trailers from YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). A trailer is loaded only when you click “Play trailer”. Before that, no data is sent to YouTube. After the click, the video is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); YouTube then receives, among other things, your IP address and may also transfer data to the USA. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your details to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). A message to media@filmpartner24.com is sufficient. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information or the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни).
