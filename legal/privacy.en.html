2. Hosting and server log files

The website is delivered via Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, Cloudflare automatically processes technical data sent by your browser (IP address, date and time, page requested, referrer, browser and operating system) in order to deliver the site and protect it against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes this data as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR on the basis of its Data Processing Addendum. For transfers to the USA, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition. The data is stored only as long as necessary for secure operation.

3. Fonts

All fonts are loaded from our own server. There is no connection to Google or other font providers.

4. Language selection, audience measurement, no tracking cookies

When you open the home page, the language is chosen automatically based on the country you are visiting from (Bulgaria: Bulgarian; Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein: German; all other countries: English). Our hosting provider Cloudflare derives the country from your IP address; nothing is stored in the process. If you choose a language yourself in the language bar, we store this choice in a cookie ("twn_lang", language code only, valid for 12 months) so that you are not redirected again. This cookie is necessary for the function you requested. Apart from this, we do not use cookies or advertising pixels. Links to sources and other websites are plain links: no data is transferred until you click a link.

Local time and weather in the date bar: To the left of the date we show the time in the capital of the country from which you are visiting, and to the right the current temperature at your approximate location. The country and approximate location (city level) are determined by our hosting provider Cloudflare from your IP address; we do not store this information or build profiles. We do not request your device's location. We fetch the weather data on our server from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no), sending only coordinates rounded to about 10 km – your IP address is not passed on to MET Norway. Weather data: MET Norway, licence CC BY 4.0. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a user-friendly presentation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Audience measurement: To learn how many people use our site, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). A small script is loaded that works without cookies and without storing anything on your device, and builds no user profiles. Only aggregated information is recorded, such as the page viewed, referring website, country, device type, browser and load time; the IP address is not stored for recognition. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in anonymous audience measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

5. Videos and trailers (YouTube)

Some articles show official trailers and news videos from broadcasters' official channels on YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). A video is loaded only when you click “Play trailer” or “Play video”. Before that, no data is sent to YouTube. After the click, the video is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); YouTube then receives, among other things, your IP address and may also transfer data to the USA. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

5a. Live market prices (TradingView)

On the “Business” page you can display a live ticker with market prices provided by TradingView, Inc. (470 Olde Worthington Road, Suite 200, Westerville, OH 43082, USA). The ticker is loaded only when you click “Live prices”; before that, no data is sent to TradingView. After the click, TradingView receives, among other things, your IP address and may also transfer data to the USA. We store your choice only locally in your browser (local storage, entry “twn_tv”) so the ticker appears directly on your next visit; you can delete this entry at any time in your browser settings. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). More information: tradingview.com/privacy-policy.

6. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your details to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information or the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни).
