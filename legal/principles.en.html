Editorial principles

Independence

TWN – World News (Terra World News) reports independently of political parties, governments and companies. The publisher is FILMPARTNER 24 EOOD. There is no advertising or paid content; should that change, it will be clearly labelled.

Sources and verification

Every report names its sources. We rely on news agencies, established media, official bodies and our own research. Information is only published if it comes from a reliable source; disputed claims are marked as such, together with who makes them.

Original texts

We do not copy texts from other media. Our reports are our own summaries and analysis. Direct quotations are placed in quotation marks and attributed to their source.

Use of artificial intelligence

The newsroom uses AI tools for research, translation and first drafts. Every report is checked, edited and approved by an editor before publication. The newsroom is responsible for all content.

Corrections

We correct errors quickly and transparently. Significant corrections are noted at the end of the report concerned. Please send any notice to media@filmpartner24.com.

News and opinion

News and commentary are clearly separated. Opinion pieces are labelled as such.
