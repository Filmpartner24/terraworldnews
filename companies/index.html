Business & Energy

Beyond the creative industries, Nedy John Cross is active in renewable energy development and in platforms for energy, science and public dialogue in South-East Europe.

CEO / Managing Director · since 2022

GREEN SOLUTION

Nedy John Cross is one of the pioneers of wind energy in Bulgaria: he was among the first to plan and build a wind farm in the country – in 2007, near Kavarna on the Black Sea coast. GREEN SOLUTION DEUTSCHLAND GmbH & GREEN SOLUTION BULGARIA Ltd. – international wind energy and infrastructure projects in Bulgaria, including the Murgana I wind farm: project development, manufacturer cooperations, international partnerships and investor communication.
