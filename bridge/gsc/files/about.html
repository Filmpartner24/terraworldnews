About us

Terra World News (TWN) is an independent online news portal. Every day, terraworldnews.com publishes current news from around the world – in Bulgarian, German and English.

What we cover

Each daily edition contains 77 stories from the sections World (including the USA), Europe, Germany, Bulgaria, Technology, Business, Climate & Energy and Entertainment, with a focus on politics in Germany and Bulgaria and the major international developments.

How we work

Every story names its sources with a link. Figures, names, quotes and dates are checked against those sources before publication; anything that cannot be verified is not published. Photos come exclusively from freely licensed sources and are credited with author and licence. More in our editorial principles.

Who we are

TWN is published by FILMPARTNER 24 EOOD; the editor-in-chief is Nedy John Cross. The newsroom works from Sofia and Berlin. TWN was founded in 2026.
