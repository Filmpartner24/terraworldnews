Section · Who we are

More than a film production.

Idea → Concept → Brand → Story → Film · Berlin

We don't just produce films. We develop ideas, brands, campaigns and stories — and bring them to life in film, design, music, sound and digital media. From the first idea through strategy, branding and storytelling to shooting, sound on set, post-production, original music, sound design and release, we create one complete creative product.

Four locations combine different strengths: Berlin — film production, media and our own production studio. Bayreuth — an internationally renowned city of culture and home of our film festival. Bulgaria — international film production, corporate communication and strategic partnerships across Southeast Europe. Los Angeles — our link to the international film industry.

FILMPARTNER24's musical expertise is an independent part of our full-service approach. Nedy John Cross, founder and creative director of FILMPARTNER24, works as a composer with Constantin Entertainment — one of the established German production companies for entertainment and audiovisual formats, with many years of experience in the German and international TV and media market.

NJC EDITION — in collaboration with Constantin Entertainment. A professional framework for composing, publishing and exploiting music for audiovisual productions.

At FILMPARTNER24, music doesn't begin once a film is fully edited. Original music can already take shape during creative development — tuned to story, dramaturgy, editing, visual language and emotion.

wpd Group × Filmpartner24

Corporate & Energy · Documentary · Campaign

For wpd Bulgaria, part of the internationally active wpd Group founded in Bremen in 1996, FILMPARTNER24 developed and produced a comprehensive audiovisual communication campaign on the subject of wind energy. The challenge: how do you turn a complex subject — energy, economy, environment, science, infrastructure, regional development and, above all, people — into a story people understand? For this, FILMPARTNER24 developed a campaign structure that combines a journalistic approach with modern corporate storytelling: one documentary for background, context and expert knowledge, accompanied by 14 campaign shorts for compact digital communication.
