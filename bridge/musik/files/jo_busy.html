Busy Signal, Egyptian Mohamed Ramadan unite on ‘Make Banger’

International artistes Busy Signal and Mohamed Ramadan have joined forces on the single Make Banger, a dynamic fusion of Egyptian pop-rap and Jamaican dancehall.

Promoters say the track, produced by Crawba Genius and Blacksatin Music, creates a powerful cultural bridge between Jamaica and the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region.

Make Banger blends Arabic and English lyrics, infectious dancehall rhythms, and contemporary urban sounds into a high-energy anthem designed to resonate with global audiences.

As Egypt serves as a key gateway to the broader MENA region, the project represents a significant opportunity to strengthen cultural connections and audience engagement between Jamaica and one of the world’s fastest-growing entertainment markets.

Promoters say Make Banger arrives at a particularly timely moment as Jamaica expands its engagement across the Middle East through tourism, investment, trade, and cultural diplomacy initiatives.

“From Kingston to Cairo, we’re showing that music has no borders. When the vibe is real, people connect to it no matter where they’re from. Working with Mohamed Ramadan on Make Banger was about bringing cultures together through music. We might speak different languages, but the energy, passion, and love for music are universal, Habibi mi fren,” said Busy Signal.

The official single is available now on major digital streaming and social media platforms worldwide.
