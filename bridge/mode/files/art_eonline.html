Watch: The Kardashian-Jenners' Halloween Party Is Worthy of the Witch and Famous

Khloe Kardashian knows how to brew up a witchin' good time.

And the proof is the Keeping Up With the Kardashians star's extravagant family Halloween party.

For the outdoor occasion, Khloe covered her backyard in a bat-covered balloon arch, skeletons riding in pool floaties and an eclectic array of painted pumpkins, as shared on Instagram Stories Oct. 23.

The ghostess with the mostest—mom to True Thompson, 8, and Tatum Thompson, 4, shared with ex Tristan Thompson—also presented her guests with a table full of candy and cookies, paired with an equally colorful fruit buffet adorned with toy spiders.

In addition, a precious shot from the party showed Khloe's youngest holding a mini haunted house created at the craft bar, with enough glitter for the entire Kardashian-Jenner clan. 

Of course, among the attendees at the spellbinding affair was sister Kourtney Kardashian, who also showed off the 42-year-old's larger-than-life decorations, including none other than Jack Skellington. Alongside a snap of the animated star from Tim Burton's macabre classic, The Nightmare Before Christmas, Kourtney boasted on Instagram Stories, "Halloween at Auntie KoKo's House."
