Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026 Brings Models, Musicians and Star Guests to Los Angeles

The Victoria's Secret Fashion Show returned in 2026 with a mix of runway veterans, musical performers and famous faces in the crowd. Gigi Hadid took part, and Barbara Palvin Sprouse walked just over a month after she and husband Dylan Sprouse welcomed their baby girl on Sept. 7.

Palvin confirmed her participation on Oct. 16, and Sprouse praised her on Instagram that day. "Proud of my missus," he wrote. "Jumping right into VS fashion show post birth. I don't know where you find the energy but damn!"

Tate McRae appeared both among the high-profile guests and among the performers, alongside Megan Moroney, Megan Thee Stallion and KATSEYE. Lisa Rinna, Vanessa Bryant and Madison LeCroy were also among the attendees at Sony Pictures Studio in Los Angeles.

WNBA star Angel Reese, who became the first WNBA player to walk the Victoria's Secret runway last year, said she would not return this time. "Unfortunately, I won't be walking this year, but I'm forever grateful that I got to live out this dream and earn my very own ANGEL WINGS!" she wrote on Instagram. "Once an ANGEL, always an ANGEL."

Originally reported by E Online!
