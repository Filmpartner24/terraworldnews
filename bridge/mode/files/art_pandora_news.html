Pandora opens world’s largest fine jewellery crafting facility

Pandora's USD 150 million investment in Vietnam increases manufacturing capacity by 50%, creates 7,000 local jobs and strengthens supply chain resilience for future growth.

1 October 2026

Pandora appoints André Branch as President of the North America cluster

André Branch brings extensive leadership experience from global consumer brands and will lead Pandora's North America operations from 15 August 2026.

4 August 2026

Pandora introduces Pandora Wonders, a new creative platform debuting with Harry Lambert

Pandora Wonders is a new long-term creative platform designed to bring fresh cultural relevance to the brand and create new reasons for consumers to engage with Pandora. Act I is a limited-edition freshwater pearl collection, co-created with renowned British stylist Harry Lambert.

1 July 2026

Pandora introduces lab-grown diamond collections in Spain and Italy

Pandora is expanding its lab-grown diamond jewellery to Spain and Italy, bringing the collections to new flagship stores in Barcelona and Milan. The launch marks another step in Pandora’s ambition to democratise diamond jewellery, making it more accessible to more people.

25 June 2026

Pandora adds carbon footprint labelling for lab-grown diamonds

New level of transparency empowers consumers to compare climate impact of their diamond jewellery. The carbon footprint of Pandora’s lab-grown diamonds is around 90% lower than mined alternatives.

6 May 2026

Pandora opens new distribution centre in Canada

The new facility will handle Pandora's growing Canadian online business and reduces the company's exposure to US tariffs

31 March 2026

Pandora enters exclusive platinum-plated jewellery

Pandora, the world's largest jewellery brand, today announces the introduction of jewellery plated with the precious metal platinum. The jewellery is certified hypoallergenic, water- and tarnish-resistant and will diversify and reduce the company's commodity exposure to rely less on silver.

4 February 2026

Pandora appoints Philippa Newman as new Chief Product Officer

Pandora today announced the appointment of Philippa Newman as the company's new Chief Product Officer, effective 9 March 2026. Philippa Newman will report directly to CEO Berta de Pablos-Barbier and will join Pandora's Executive Leadership Team.
