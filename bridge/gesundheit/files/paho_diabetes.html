World Diabetes Day Webinar: Early Action on Diabetes

Register here

Join us on November 11, 2026, at 11:00 am (EST) on the webinar "Early Action on Diabetes" organized by the Pan American Health Organization to mark World Diabetes Day. The session will highlight highlight the importance of early action, share experiences and best practices in primary health care, and promote strategies to close gaps in diagnosis and care, in line with the PAHO Better Care for NCDs initiative. 

The event will feature new technical resources from PAHO, including a framework to monitor diabetes and other NCDs in primary health care facilities, the new English version of the course "Caring for People with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in PHC Settings," along with national experiences aimed at strengthening diabetes care.

This webinar aims to reinforce the regional commitment to improving the response to this disease, highlighting the role of primary health care in timely diagnosis and access to comprehensive, equitable, and high-quality care. 

RECORDING

Objectives: 

  • Present the English version of the PAHO virtual course on diabetes, aimed at primary care teams.
  • To highlight the challenges and opportunities in promoting early action on diabetes in the Region of the Americas, aligned with the 2030 global coverage targets.
  • To raise awareness of the critical role of primary health care and the "Better Care for NCDs" initiative in enhancing timely diagnosis and providing comprehensive diabetes care. 
  • To emphasize the importance of monitoring key indicators to identify gaps in diabetes diagnosis, access, and management, which will aid in decision-making to enhance health care outcomes.
  • Share country perspectives and experiences in strengthening comprehensive diabetes management in primary care.
