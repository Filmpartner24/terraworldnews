Summerfest announcements carry on with Kansas, Night Ranger

Matt Wild
November 16, 2026

It’s great when Summerfest announces headliners like Mumford & Sons and Death Cab For Cutie. But it’s also great when Summerfest announces headliners like Foreigner and, today, Kansas and Night Ranger. THAT’S the Summerfest we all know and love!

Anyway, Kansas and Night Ranger will headline the BMO Pavilion on Friday, July 9. Reserved-seating tickets go on sale Friday, November 20 at 10 a.m. Get them at Summerfest.com, Ticketmaster.com, or in person at the Summerfest Box Office. General admission seats at the back of the BMO Pavilion are free.

Summerfest’s 2027 dates are June 24-26, July 1-3, and July 8-10. Here’s “Sister Christian.”
