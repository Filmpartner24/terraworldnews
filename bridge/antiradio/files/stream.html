Anti Radio Live Stream — Listen Free, 24/7

Welcome to the Anti Radio live stream. This is where the music lives: an ad-free, algorithm-free online radio broadcast from Baku, Azerbaijan, playing around the clock to listeners all over the world. Press play above and you’re in. No account, no app, no interruptions.

How to listen in your favourite app

The Anti Radio live stream works with virtually every player that supports internet radio. A few of the most popular:

  • VLC Media Player
    MediaOpen Network Stream Paste the URL. Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.
  • Winamp / foobar2000
    FileOpen URL Or Add Location, then paste the link.
  • Apple Music / iTunes
    FileOpen Stream URL Paste the URL.
  • Radio apps
    Search for “Anti Radio” in apps like Radio Garden, TuneIn, or myTuner, or add the stream URL manually as a custom station.
  • Smart speakers & car systems
    Most Android Auto and CarPlay-compatible radio apps let you save a custom stream URL.
  • Home audio
    Sonos, Volumio, and most network receivers accept a direct MP3 stream link under “custom radio” or “TuneIn URL”.

Once saved, the stream behaves like any other station: one tap and you’re listening.

Listen in your browser

Don’t want to install anything? The web player at the top of this page runs on any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — on desktop or phone. Open it in a separate window to keep the music going while you browse the rest of the site.

The player also shows what’s playing right now, the recent track history, and a live listener count, with quick links to find each song on Spotify or YouTube.

What you’ll hear

Every time you connect to the Anti Radio live stream, you get a freshly shuffled mix. Deep house and house are at the core of the rotation — tune in to Deep House Radio or House Radio — and around them it moves freely across indie, electronic, alternative, pop, and hip-hop, chosen by real people, not by charts or engagement metrics. Learn more about how we pick our music on the About Us page, and explore the musicians we play in our artist profiles.

Want to hear your own taste on air? Send us a link to your Spotify or Yandex Music playlist at info@antiradio.net and we’ll weave it into the rotation.

Stream quality & reliability

The broadcast runs on dedicated streaming infrastructure with global content delivery, so latency stays low whether you’re in Baku, Berlin, or Boston. Uptime is monitored around the clock. If the stream ever drops, it reconnects automatically in the web player; in third-party apps, simply press play again.

Troubleshooting

No sound?
Check that your browser tab isn’t muted and that autoplay is allowed for antiradio.net.
Buffering?
Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data (or vice versa), or try the direct stream URL in VLC.
Stream not found in an app?
Make sure you pasted the full URL including https://.

Still stuck? Email info@antiradio.net and we’ll help.
