Agrivoltaics: One Acre, Three Jobs

 

 

Solar panels and tomatoes may not seem like an obvious pair. At UC Davis though, they share the same field. Agrivoltaics is the practice of using solar panels in farm fields, creating energy while growing crops. Researchers say the solar panels have another job: saving farmers water by shielding crops from extreme heat. In this episode of Unfold, we step into a tomato field turned climate experiment to see what growers and scientists are weighing as the planet warms.

In this episode:

  • Majdi Abou Najm, UC Davis professor of soil biophysics
  • Gavin Chaboya, UC Davis Ph.D. student in Abou Najm’s lab
  • Kevin Logue, business development director at Sunlight Energy
  • Greg Roth, walnut farmer in Winters, Calif.
  • Bruce Rominger, farmer in Yolo County, Calif.

Read an in-depth In Focus feature story on agrivoltaics.
