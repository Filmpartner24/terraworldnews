    Eleftheria Zeggini Receives FEBS | EMBO Women in Science Award 2027

    Prof. Eleftheria Zeggini has been awarded the FEBS | EMBO Women in Science Award 2027 for her pioneering contributions to human genomics and the translation of large-scale genetic discoveries into biological and clinical insights, alongside her scientific leadership and sustained commitment to mentoring, equality and inclusion.
