EPFL labs can now measure CO₂ emissions and focus reduction efforts

13.10.26 NewsEPFL

EPFL has developed a carbon emissions calculator that helps research labs better target their reduction efforts. It gives a customized, granular view of a lab’s carbon emissions so that scientists can see where they should focus their attention. The open-source program is designed for use at other universities, too.

Bacteria use collisions to navigate crowded environments

12.10.26 NewsResearch

Some bacteria can sense when they bump into each other and use these interactions to shape how the group organizes and moves. This discovery reveals a new way microbes explore their environments using physical feedback rather than chemical signals.
